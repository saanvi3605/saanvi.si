Research

First author · under review

ESI-H: A Hazard-Based Economic Stress Index

A hazard-based recession-risk index with a real-time cycle clock and an implied recession horizon, built from macroeconomic signals and validated out of sample.

Out-of-sample AUC 0.919 / PR-AUC 0.569 vs 0.613 / 0.132 for prior work.
Project-I · patent in progress

RACRP: Retrieval-Augmented Cold-Start Risk Prediction for Serverless

Conditional idle-gap histograms plus a cost-derived warm window, replacing the fixed percentile rule used in production keep-alive policies. Evaluated on the Azure Functions 2019 trace.

78.8% fewer cold starts than Azure's ATC'20 policy at matched memory cost, across 1,999 apps on held-out days.
BMC Genomics · under review

BioTool: A Comprehensive Survey of DNA Repeat Detection Methods

A survey of DNA repeat and motif detection tools, paired with Seqtor — an interactive catalogue I built of ~350 tools with comparative metadata and network visualisation.

Risk-Aware Human-in-the-Loop Framework for Trustworthy LLM-Assisted Data Repair

Routes every LLM-proposed repair to auto-repair, abstain, or human review to minimise expected total cost — using an XGBoost verifier and constraint-graph impact scores on the REIN benchmark.
